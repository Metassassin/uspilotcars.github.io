Pilot Car Regulations

New Mexico Pilot Car Regulations

Guidelines for New Mexico pilot car, escort car, and flag car regulations. Always confirm current requirements with the permit office.

Verify before you move: These are guidelines only and are subject to change without notice. Always verify current requirements against the permit issued by the pertinent permit office before a move.

Quick Facts

When a permit is neededOver 8'6" wide, over 14' high, or trailer over 57'6" long
Special information neededInsurance must be on file with the permit office
Times of movementSunrise to sunset, Monday through Saturday. No travel Sundays.
Holiday restrictionsNo movement on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, or Christmas. If a holiday falls on a day other than Monday or Saturday, the restriction runs from sunset the day before through sunrise the day after. If it falls on a Monday, movement is prohibited from sunset the preceding Saturday until sunrise the following Tuesday. If it falls on a Saturday, movement is prohibited from sunset the preceding Friday until sunrise the following Monday.
CurfewsAlbuquerque & Espanola: 7-9am and 4-6pm. Santa Fe: 7-9am and 11:30am-1:30pm and 4-6pm.

When a Pilot / Escort Vehicle Is Required

Official reference: state administrative code ↗

  • Over 8'6" wide, if deemed a hazard: 1 pilot vehicle
  • Over 14' wide: 1 pilot vehicle in front on 2-lane roads, in rear on 4-lane roads
  • Over 20' wide for 5+ miles: police escort plus flagman
  • Over 90' up to 110' long: 1 pilot vehicle in front on 2-lane roads, in rear on 4-lane roads
  • Over 110' long: 2 pilot vehicles, front and rear
  • 16' and over in height: 1 escort with height pole in front (plus an extra person or pilot vehicle to check overhead clearance)
  • 18' and over in height: 1 escort with height pole in front and 1 pilot vehicle in rear (plus clearance coordination with utilities and local authorities)

Equipment Requirements

Vehicle: Must be DOT-inspected annually with certification carried at all NM ports of entry. Wheelbase at least 100 inches; must not exceed 1.5 ton capacity. Insurance minimum 25/50/10, proof carried in the vehicle.

Signs: "OVERSIZE LOAD" sign, 5' x 1' minimum, black letters 10" x 1" on a yellow background, mounted front and rear or on top of the vehicle.

Lights: 2 rotating amber lights (rotating, flashing, or strobe) mounted on top at vehicle width (not to exceed 8'), visible at 1,000 feet minimum in normal sunlight.

Flags: 2 red or fluorescent orange flags, minimum 12" per side, mounted front and rear or on top.

Additional equipment: Two-way radio; rear-view mirrors on both sides; one fire extinguisher (2.5 lb CO2 or dry chemical or equivalent); identification signage showing company name, address, and state authority number on both sides (removable magnetic signs are acceptable); 3 red flares or reflectors.

Operator Requirements

At least 18 years of age.
